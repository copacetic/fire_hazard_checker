How to read this: insurers don't publish per-address eligibility, but they underwrite from the same official hazard maps shown here, plus proprietary wildfire scores (brush distance, slope, road access, roof type). Zone status is the strongest public predictor of whether carriers will quote — the definitive answer is always a real quote, which the links above start.
